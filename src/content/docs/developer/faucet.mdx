The Dusk testnet faucet sends free testnet DUSK to your wallet on the [Nocturne testnet](/operator/networks/#nocturne-testnet). Use it to try transfers, stake on a testnet node, bridge to DuskEVM Testnet, or deploy smart contracts. Testnet DUSK has no real-world value.

:::note[What you need]
- A Discord account that has joined the [Dusk Discord server](https://discord.gg/dusk-official).
- A Dusk testnet wallet address. Create one in the [testnet Web Wallet](https://apps.testnet.dusk.network/wallet/), or set up [Rusk Wallet](/use/wallets/#rusk-wallet) for testnet.
:::

## Request testnet DUSK

1. Open the [Dusk Discord server](https://discord.gg/dusk-official).
2. In the member list, find the bot **Dusk Testnet Faucet** among the team members.
3. Right-click the bot and select **Message**.
4. Send this command:

   ```text
   !dusk
   ```

5. When the bot asks for your address, reply with your testnet wallet address.
6. Check the balance in your wallet on testnet.

Each request sends 5,000 testnet DUSK, and you can make 1 request per Discord user and wallet every 24 hours. The bot queues your transaction. It usually reaches your wallet within a few minutes, and can take longer when many people are using the faucet. If it still hasn't arrived, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A second request in the same day doesn't go through | Wait until 24 hours have passed since your last request, then try again. |
| The DUSK hasn't arrived | Check that you sent a **testnet** address and that your wallet is set to testnet. During busy periods, the queue takes longer. |
| You need DUSK in more than one wallet | Send part of your balance from the first wallet to the second, or request again for the other wallet after 24 hours. |
| Something else went wrong | Ask in the [official Dusk community channels](/learn/community/). |
