Use Dusk's official channels to get updates, ask questions, and contribute. [Stay safe](/learn/stay-safe/#official-channels) lists each channel with its exact address.

## Getting help

- For documentation fixes and requests, contribute in the docs repo: <a href="https://github.com/dusk-network/docs" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">dusk-network/docs</a>.
- For protocol/app questions, use [Discord](https://discord.gg/dusk-official).
- Be careful with DMs and links. The Dusk team will never DM you first or ask for your recovery phrase or private keys. See [Stay safe](/learn/stay-safe/).
