Anyone can pretend to be part of the Dusk team on X, Discord, Telegram, or by email. A list of team accounts can't verify who is behind an account, and it goes out of date, so these docs don't keep one. Use the rules below instead.

## What the Dusk team will never do

The Dusk team will never:

- Contact you first by direct message (DM).
- Ask for your recovery phrase (the words that restore your wallet), private keys, or passwords.
- Ask you to send funds, or to "verify" your wallet or your funds.
- Offer support, airdrops, or giveaways in DMs.
- Ask you to install software or connect your wallet through a link in a DM.

Anyone who does one of these things isn't from Dusk, whatever their name, photo, or handle says.

## How to spot impersonation

- **Look-alike handles and domains.** Check every character. A fake account or site may add or swap a letter, use a 0 for an o, add an underscore or a word like "official" or "support", or use a different domain ending.
- **Copied names and photos.** Anyone can copy a team member's display name and profile photo. On Discord, display names aren't unique, and usernames can change hands.
- **Urgency and pressure.** A message that says your wallet will be locked, your funds are at risk, or an offer ends soon is trying to rush you.
- **A move to private messages.** Someone answers your question in a public channel, then asks you to continue in DMs or to open a "support ticket" through a link.

## What to do

1. Don't reply, click links, connect your wallet, or sign anything.
2. Report the account to the platform as impersonation.
3. Tell Dusk through an official channel, for example in [Discord](https://discord.gg/dusk-official) or by email to [hello@dusk.network](mailto:hello@dusk.network).

:::caution
If you already shared your recovery phrase or a private key, assume someone else can move your funds. Create a new wallet with a new recovery phrase and move your funds to it right away.
:::
